About Us

Technology to improve healthcare

The Corporation has been incorporated through legislative measures by the state Govt. of Punjab to bring more administrative flexibility for implementation of the "Second State Health Systems Development Project" with World Bank assistance to upgrade Health Services at secondary level.

The Corporation has taken over 150 Institutions which includes District Hospitals, Sub-Divisional Hospitals and Community Health Centres. The 86 Medical Institutions are situated in rural areas and 64 are in Urban areas.
