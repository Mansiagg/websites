About Us

Provide best quality healthcare for you!

The Corporation was established by the Government of Punjab through legislative measures to enhance administrative flexibility for the "Second State Health Systems Development Project." With support from the World Bank, we aim to upgrade health services at the secondary level. Currently, the Corporation manages 150 institutions, including District Hospitals, Sub-Divisional Hospitals, and Community Health Centres—with 86 facilities located in rural areas and 64 in urban centers.
