Drug and Vaccine Distribution
Management System (DVDMS)

An Online Supply chain management in the health sector involving the planning, procurement, distribution, and management of pharmaceuticals, medical supplies, and equipment to ensure the availability of essential health products. Designed and Developed by CDAC, Noida

DVDMS As A
Technology Solution

Real-time tracking and visibility of the supply chain.

Using data analytics to forecast demand, optimize inventory, and improve overall efficiency

Incorporating electronic health records to enhance communication and information flow.

Procurement & Sourcing
Through DVDMS

Identifying and selecting suppliers for pharmaceuticals, medical equipment, and supplies.

Negotiating contracts and ensuring compliance with quality standards.

Managing relationships with suppliers to ensure timely deliveries.
