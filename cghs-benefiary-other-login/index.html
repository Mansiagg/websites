Comprehensive Medical Care

CGHS beneficiaries are entitled to outpatient consultations, specialist services, and inpatient treatment through CGHS Wellness Centres and empanelled hospitals, ensuring quality healthcare across India.

Affordable and Cashless Treatment

Beneficiaries can avail cashless treatment at empanelled private hospitals for approved procedures, with medicines and diagnostics provided at nominal or no cost as per CGHS guidelines.

Wide Coverage for Family Members

CGHS covers eligible family members of the beneficiary, including dependents and pensioners, offering continuous healthcare support and financial relief throughout different stages of life.
